Scope and interpretation

520 histories, 480 physical configurations, ten grouped rotations: eight folds train, one validation, one test. Seed replicas stay together. Activity is defined from simulation history using frozen engineering thresholds, not a physical extinction claim. Gates can reopen; heating remains ungated. Both models remain unconstrained and can predict invalid negative quantities or increasing material mass.

Generic 3 m wide vegetation cones, heights 4/6/8/10 m above a 2 m base, 1,600 weighted particles, 0.5 m grid in a 24×16×16 m domain, 180 s histories and an imposed 1500°C ignition source for 20 s. No trunks, buildings or embers; 48 legacy histories retain their documented geometry. Water/fuel are derived with a 0.75 fuel-gas yield; char is treated as inert ash. These results are not physical validation.
