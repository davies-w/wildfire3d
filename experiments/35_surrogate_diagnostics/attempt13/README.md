# Full-dataset compact-state sequence learning

Requested grouped 10-fold cross-validation over all 520 archived histories (480 physical configurations). Each rotation has 384 training, 48 validation and 48 test configurations; seed replicas stay together. Former test labels are retained only as provenance; these are newly held out within this experiment, not historically pristine tests. No data exclusion or 48-case fallback is permitted. Splits use physical configuration keys, not filenames or individual snapshots, and do not use targets.

Input: one 52-component evolving state plus 13 external/fixed components (65 total); output: the next 52-component state. The state comprises 9 gas/source/heat summaries, 5 quantities for each of 4 material classes (20), HOT and HRR region presence/centroid/symmetric covariance (10 each), and 3 HOT-weighted gas velocities. The external input includes height, loading, initial moisture, sine/cosine ignition angle, ambient +X wind, initial four material masses, ambient temperature, fraction of the timestep with imposed ignition on, and actual timestep. Crown width3m and base2m are fixed in this dataset. Ambient wind is supplied, not forecast. The state is not true thermal energy; material mass is not separated water/dryfuel/char. See schema.json for units and encoding scales.

Residual MLP: 65 -> 128 SiLU -> 128 SiLU -> 52, zero-initialized last layer starts as persistence. Twenty one-step epochs precede eight-step differentiable sequence training. All consecutive transitions are covered in nonoverlapping windows, including a shorter final window. Each sampled window starts at a measured state; subsequent steps feed predictions back. Full autonomous validation starts only from the first measured state and uses no later truth resets. Actual future ignition command and fixed wind are known external forcing. Every epoch evaluates full train and validation one-step and autonomous errors. Normalize using training data only, with configuration-balanced moments and replica-balanced losses. Undefined plume geometry/velocities are explicitly masked, not scored as fake zero geometry.

Maximum400 epochs; select minimum full-rollout validation objective, with patience30 after at least50 epochs and relative0.1%/absolute1e-5 meaningful improvement. Selection considers every trained epoch, including warmup. Patience resets when sequence training begins at epoch21; warmup cannot cause early termination. Checkpoints include optimizer, fold normalization, RNG, validation selection and full learning history every five completed epochs and at fold termination; an interrupted run resumes only from a preserved completed epoch under a separately reserved attempt. A deadline or epoch cap is not convergence. Only after all ten models are selected are their test folds scored. Test results never select checkpoints, scales, architecture or stopping. Keep final and selected checkpoints separate. Report ten learning curves, physical per-variable errors, persistence baselines, pooled out-of-fold test errors and fold variability. No conservation, nonnegativity or positive-semidefinite covariance constraint is claimed; violations and numerical divergence must be reported, not hidden by clipping predictions.

This is a compact-state sufficiency experiment, not a demonstrated FDS replacement. The gas-HRR summary integrates the positive part of saved HRRPUV over0.125m³ cells; global CSV HRR remains a distinct signed quantity. Its time integral is released energy in kJ, not stored gas thermal energy. Compact values are cast to float32 for training. Every epoch records per-feature physical MAE in ignition/post-source-to90s/after90s windows, released-energy error and HOT-presence precision/recall/F1; final reports add per-case errors and invalid-state diagnostics. Undefined plume features are masked from scoring, while a presence feature remains scored. Predictions have no truth-dependent mask during autonomous rollout.

This uses predicted presence, never truth, during rollout and prevents undefined coordinates becoming an unreported memory state. Direct loss still scores raw candidates wherever truth defines the quantity, so a missed presence prediction cannot evade the geometry loss. Moment MAEs are target-active candidate errors; interpret alongside presence precision/recall.


## Full-dataset compact-state sequences (attempt13, completed 23 October)

All 520 histories / 480 physical configurations were prepared without exclusions: 47,320 snapshots and 46,800 transitions. Ten grouped rotations used 384 training, 48 validation and 48 test configurations; replicas stayed together. Each configuration was tested once, after all ten validation-selected checkpoints were frozen. These reshuffled simulations were previously examined and are not historically pristine tests.

The residual MLP has 65 inputs (52 evolving summaries plus 13 external/static components), two 128-unit SiLU hidden layers, and 52 next-state outputs; 31,668 parameters. Twenty one-step warmup epochs preceded eight-step backpropagation. Full 180-second autonomous rollouts and one-step metrics were evaluated on training and validation every epoch. Normalization used only each fold's training configurations.

All ten folds stopped by validation patience after 94–196 epochs, selecting epochs 64–166, rather than reaching a budget or epoch cap. Curves show large initial improvement followed by fluctuating, flatter training/validation errors; this meets the declared stopping rule, not proof of physical fidelity or globally sufficient optimization. The equal-configuration held-out rollout normalized MSE was 0.12357, versus mean fold persistence MSE 3.04461 (about 95.9% lower). Persistence keeps the initial state unchanged and is a weak baseline. One-step fold errors were 0.0121–0.0142, while autonomous errors were 0.0944–0.1479: recursive error accumulation remains substantial. This is not a controlled comparison against earlier volume models because outputs, normalization and splits differ.

Physical full-history, configuration-balanced rollout MAEs were 713.60 kW gas heat release, 21.06 m3 HOT volume, and 58.15 C peak gas temperature. All 480 configurations had finite reported aggregate errors. However, negative gas-HRR predictions occupied 36.1–64.2% of evaluated predictions across folds; invalid active plume covariance matrices occupied 51.0–81.1%. These fractions alone do not specify violation magnitude, but negative heat release and covariance matrices that cannot describe a real plume invalidate use as a physical simulator. No silent clipping was applied. The unconstrained model learned useful temporal signal but is not a usable FDS replacement. The data do not establish that these compact summaries are a sufficient Markov state.

All ten learning curves and 52 per-feature curve grids are in attempt13/data/visuals; per-window physical errors, released gas energy, HOT presence, per-case tests and persistence results remain in the archived report. Material mass is not separately dry fuel/water/char; temperature-excess volume is not stored thermal energy in joules. Generic conical crowns and the artificial 1500 C, 20-second source, 0.5 m grid, absence of trunks/buildings/embers and lack of real-world validation remain consequential limitations.
