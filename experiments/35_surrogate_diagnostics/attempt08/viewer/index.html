What this comparison means

All archived new-geometry train, validation and previously examined test cases share the same condition sliders. A missing combination is left empty: no nearest-case substitution or invented truth. Additional seeds of training configurations were not fitted by these models. Prediction scores use the fixed 0.5 decision threshold; they are not calibrated probabilities.

CPU inference can differ slightly from the training GPU near the fixed decision threshold: on400 shared runs, confusion-count entries differed by at most6 cells out of49,152. Equal counts do not establish identical masks. These are precomputed outputs of frozen final checkpoints, selected by exact recorded conditions—not a neural network running in the browser. Truth and prediction use full 0.5m cells in a 24 × 16 × 16m domain. Boxes show thresholded gas cells, not flame surfaces. Grey/green cells show initial weighted fuel-particle occupancy, not trunks or solid foliage.

Generic 3m-wide conical crowns, 4/6/8/10m tall above 2m; 1600 weighted particles. Wind fixed along +X. Artificial 1500°C source for 20s; no trunks, buildings or embers. Independent snapshot classifiers have no evolving fuel state and no guaranteed consistency across times. These comparisons do not establish real-world fire accuracy.
