# Experiment 30: autonomous single-tree sequence pilot

Run the fixed-tree sequence test, evaluate autonomous drift, then use its findings to design tree-geometry/fuel-loading variations. Stage 1 reuses 32 training and six development-validation configurations; no prior test cases. No new FDS simulations in this first stage. Snapshot benchmarks remain frozen.

Model: 2-second recursive transitions, six gas/radiation outputs on the original 0.5m grid (48x32x32), a 2m internal recurrent/material grid and a decoder back to 0.5m. Inputs include four class-specific frozen vegetation mass-density maps, ignition-particle footprint/on-off timing, fixed moisture, wind, spatial coordinates, previous predicted fields, remaining material fraction and particle temperature. Intermediate grids are learned numerical representations, not additional FDS simulation resolutions. Initial fields are ambient 20C, uniform prescribed wind and ambient integrated radiation; FDS's tiny initial velocity perturbations are omitted. During autonomous inference, no later FDS states or incoming heat values are provided.

Raw particle evidence supervises remaining wet+solid mass and mass-weighted particle temperature. Mass is not unburned dry fuel: it includes water/solid chemistry. Monotone mass depletion is a modeling assumption and is audited. Initial geometry and material quantities are fixed setup descriptors. Because every existing case uses one identical tree, geometry-input generalization cannot be claimed here.

Physical setup: generic cone crown 3m diameter and 8m height at z=2–10m, 1600 frozen vegetation particles, fixed 0.12 moisture, wind +X 1–5m/s and varying ignition azimuth. Artificial stationary 1500C ignition particles last20s. 24x16x16m domain, bare nonflammable ground; no trunks, buildings or embers. Radiation channel is integrated intensity, not directional wall heat flux. The six field outputs do not directly represent ground-surface temperature.

Training uses short rollout sequences, with progressively longer prediction windows, seeded with training states only. Validation and reported autonomous inference start from prescribed ambient conditions and recurse for180s. Teacher-forced diagnostics are separately labeled. Predeclared useful improvement: >=5% lower mean full-history physical objective than the previous snapshot model, no more than10% degradation in peak-temperature MAE, stable finite rollouts, and report all channel tradeoffs. Validation-selected results are development evidence, not fresh-test validation. A failed sequence pilot does not justify claiming composition or physical validity.

GPU stage operational ceiling $1, no automatic retries. Full optimizer/model/RNG/best-state checkpoints every200 updates, with next-step restoration test. Runtime deadline includes startup; cloud-side cleanup operates without laptop. No laptop training.
