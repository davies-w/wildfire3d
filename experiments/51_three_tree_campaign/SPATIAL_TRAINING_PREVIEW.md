# Two-case spatial training previews

Only tree51_001323_baseline and tree51_001336_baseline are converted. Original previews and raw simulation data remain unchanged. Bulk conversion and deletion await user review.

The source-anchored cone fixes its apex to the imposed ignition contact location and uses a full 3D unit direction, length and radius. Its 11 channels comprise the original four water/fuel/gas/heating channels plus presence, three direction components, length, radius and peak temperature. The anchor is a fixed input, not a learned fire origin. The cone may extend outside the simulated domain; there is no observed evidence outside that domain.

The free ellipsoid uses a temperature-excess-weighted centroid and covariance, scaled to cover at least 95% of cells above 50 C. It is not a minimum-volume fit. Its 15 channels comprise the original four, presence, three centre coordinates, six symmetric shape-matrix entries and peak temperature. The shape matrix defines (x-c)^T inverse(S) (x-c)<=1; entries have units m². The centre describes hot-gas location, not the origin of combustion.

Each dataset has 91 rows, including six saved-volume fits and 85 interpolated spatial rows. All four original state histories are retained exactly. Interpolation cannot recover transient motion or unsaved peaks. At appearance/disappearance, position or direction is held at the available active endpoint while extents shrink or grow linearly; ellipsoid matrix entries scale quadratically. This explicit synthetic transition avoids moving a centre from an absent zero origin. Active-to-active ellipsoid matrix interpolation is linear; cone direction uses normalized linear interpolation. No extrapolation. Source values, timestamps, masks, bracket indices and weights are retained. Absent geometry and peak values are zero sentinels, not physical ground locations or 0 C.

Animations read the saved training NPZ files directly. Each frame corresponds to one row. Gray dots show a display subsample of actual hot cells only at saved-volume times. Wire shapes are geometry, not a temperature field. Green outlines show initial vegetation, not remaining fuel; the red star is the fixed ignition location, not an indicator that the ignitor remains on. Display playback is accelerated with longer pauses at observed frames. Oak has no >50 C cells at any saved volume; this does not exclude a transient between the saved times, including the imposed 20-second ignition.

These are generic-fuel, species-inspired geometries on a 0.5 m grid, with imposed 1500 C contact ignition. No species calibration, bark, embers, grass or resolved solid trunk is established. See the original preview metadata for fuel bookkeeping and geometry limitations. Coverage is a fitting criterion, not predictive accuracy.

Outputs: data/spatial-training-variants/CASE/{cone,ellipsoid}/{training.npz,training.csv,metadata.json,first-transition.json,first-transition.md}. Each first-transition example contains fixed inputs and exactly the first and next state, without a hidden history window. Paired animations are CASE/comparison-3d.gif. The frozen DVC stage is spatial_training_variants_two_cases.
