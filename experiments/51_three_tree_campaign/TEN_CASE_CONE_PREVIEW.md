# Ten additional random cone comparisons

This extends the approved 11-channel ignition-anchored cone and 14-channel freely fitted cone to the same ten additional cases. Selection is recorded in ten-case-cone-selection.json: Python random seed 20261109, uniform sampling without replacement from 264 verified 180-second Attempt 7 cases with local extraction inputs and passed raw backup receipts, excluding the original two. This is a convenience population of currently available backed-up cases, not a random sample of all 2,000 planned scenarios. No outcome filtering or substitution of selected cases is allowed.

Final raw archives are restored from HF and their SHA-256 values checked. Extraction also checks every needed raw member against local input bytes and the completed-case proof. The original seven vegetation/stem classes and exclusion of the ignitor are unchanged. Four water/fuel/gas/heating channels remain identical between representations. Both cone-fit algorithms, bounds, threshold, coverage criterion, interpolation rules and units remain those of the corrected two-case comparison in CONE_COMPARISON_PREVIEW.md.

Each paired 3D animation reads its training arrays directly. Initial pine conical crowns, broadleaf ellipsoidal crowns and stems are display context, not burn state. Gray hot-cell dots appear only at actual volume observations. Wire cones between observations are interpolated geometry, never recovered raw frames. Free-cone apex is geometric, not a physical ignition origin. No new simulations or model training; all raw and training data remain local. The older two-case datasets are retained.

Outputs are data/ten-case-cone-preview/CASE/{cone,free_cone}, with NPZ/CSV training arrays, metadata and first-transition examples; CASE/comparison-3d.gif contains both 3D views. base-inputs retains the four-state extraction provenance and original sampled data; anchored-inputs retains intermediate anchored-cone provenance. fit-report.json records raw-frame fits. All these artifacts are preserved together, but only cone and free_cone are the requested training representations.
