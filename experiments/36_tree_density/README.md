## Retrospective undershoot and smoothness audit (2026-10-27)

The audit covers the original gated ten-fold gallery (520 histories / 480 configurations), denser plain ten-fold gallery (2,080 / 2,040), and the locally collected stride-one shared-test pair (208 / 204). This is post-hoc diagnosis on already examined tests, not a new accuracy benchmark, retraining, causal inference, or physical validation. Replicas are averaged within configuration. A large gas undershoot is ranked by maximum positive (truth minus prediction), divided by that history's absolute truth peak; this is not a pointwise relative error near zero. The two four-state models predict aggregates, not arbitrary spatial fire fields.

`results/gallery-undershoot-diagnosis.json` analyzes exactly the displayed curves, including the first15 autonomous targets followed by refreshed16-step forecasts (~32s). In the stride-one gated arm,20 of21 top-decile gas failures have their maximum deficit in the initial~32s. Their median predicted-peak amplitude bias is -43.7%, and median absolute peak-time offset is6.0s: both amplitude and timing matter. The30%-moisture group has23.4% top-decile membership (47configurations), versus2.6% for6%-moisture (39configurations). These moisture ratios are initial water/dry fuel, not green area in the renderer. Wind5m/s has21.7% membership (23configs) versus6.7% at1m/s (45configs). Angle0°/315° has29.2%/25.8% membership (24/31configs);135°/225° has zero in34/27configs. Fuel loading changes interpretation with the forecast regime.
